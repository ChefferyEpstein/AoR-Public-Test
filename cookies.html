Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Ace of Raves uses cookies and similar technologies that store information on, or access information from, your device.

2. Strictly necessary technologies

Our website may use strictly necessary storage to provide features you request, such as keeping track of a shopping cart, maintaining a checkout/session, remembering essential security state and preventing abuse.

The current custom Ace of Raves storefront may store a cart identifier in your browser so the cart can persist between page loads. This is used to provide the shopping service rather than for advertising.

3. Analytics, advertising and other non-essential technologies

At launch, we do not intend to activate non-essential analytics or advertising technologies on the custom Ace of Raves storefront without first implementing the required choice/consent controls.

If we later add analytics, advertising pixels, personalisation or other non-essential storage/access technologies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent before those technologies are used.

4. Third-party checkout and services

When you use a third-party service such as Shopify checkout, that service may use cookies or similar technologies necessary to provide its service and may provide additional information about its own use of data and cookies.

5. Managing your choices

If a cookie preference control is displayed on the site, you can use it to manage non-essential technologies. You can also control cookies through your browser, although blocking strictly necessary technologies may stop parts of the shop or checkout from working correctly.

6. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies or similar technologies can be sent to ace@aceofraves.co.uk.
