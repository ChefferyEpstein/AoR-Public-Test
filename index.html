Why Ace of Raves exists

Built for the love of the rave.

Ace of Raves wasn’t built to squeeze as much money as possible out of a T-shirt. It was built for the culture, the music and the people around it — and to grow into something that can support the artists and people keeping the scene moving.

Like any business, we’ve got overheads. Garments, printing, fulfilment and keeping the lights on all cost money. But we don’t think you should need a ridiculous amount of disposable income just to wear something decent to a rave.

So the range is built around choice. Don’t Break the Bank, get Some Real Gourmet Shit, or Blow the Roof Off the Bank. Same AoR artwork — you choose how fancy the thing underneath it needs to be.

The highest-quality organic option we can reasonably source. Because we have to look after the planet — we all rave here.

The end goal is to source increasingly sustainable ravewear at fair prices, support artists as Ace of Raves grows, and eventually give a share of profits back through charitable donations. First we’ve got to make some profits to give away.

Make good shit. Keep it fair. Keep it accessible. Look after the scene. Look after the planet.
